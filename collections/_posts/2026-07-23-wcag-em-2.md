[WCAG Evaluation Methodology (WCAG-EM) 2.0 ](https://www.w3.org/TR/wcag-em-2/) is published as a W3C Group Note. WCAG-EM describes a methodology with a step-by-step process to evaluate how well digital products conform to Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2. WCAG-EM 1 was specifically for testing websites and web pages. WCAG-EM 2 also applies to apps and other digital products. For an introduction to WCAG-EM, see [WCAG-EM Overview](/test-evaluate/conformance/wcag-em/).
