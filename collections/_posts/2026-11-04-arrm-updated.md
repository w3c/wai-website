---
title: "Accessibility Roles and Responsibilities Mapping (ARRM) updated"
date: 2026-11-04
---

[Accessibility Roles and Responsibilities Mapping (ARRM)](https://www.w3.org/WAI/planning/arrm/) is updated. ARRM helps you assign responsibilities for digital accessibility to appropriate roles early in projects. The resource now provides an approach for WCAG 2.2 success criteria. For the complete list of changes, see [Changelog for ARRM](/planning/arrm/changelog/). If you would like to be involved in developing ARRM, we encourage you to join the [ARRM Community Group](https://www.w3.org/community/arrm/).
